# Quanta Katenet: Validator Rewards Program (Official)

Welcome to the Quanta Katenet! This document outlines the rewards structure for the founding node operators who help us secure and test the network before Mainnet launch.

## 1. The Challenge
Currently, to run a Validator on the Quanta network, an operator must stake a minimum of **100,000 QUA**. 

Because Quanta is launching fairly, we will **not** be giving away millions of QUA for free. Instead, we are using a **Foundation Delegation Model** to reward our Katenet operators.

## 2. The Rewards
If you run a Katenet node and maintain high uptime during the testnet phase, you will earn two things at the Mainnet Token Generation Event (TGE):

### Reward A: The Token Grant (Owned by You)
Every successful Katenet validator will receive a direct airdrop of **5,000 QUA** at Mainnet launch. 
- You fully own these tokens.
- They will be vested linearly over 12 months.

### Reward B: The Foundation Delegation (Your Mainnet Ticket)
To ensure you can actually run a node on Mainnet without having to buy 100,000 QUA, the Quanta Foundation will **delegate exactly 100,000 QUA** to your Mainnet validator node for a period of **1 Year**.
- **What this means for you:** You will be instantly elected into the active Validator set (Top 21) on Mainnet Day 1. You will earn and keep 100% of the block rewards (0.5 QUA per block + transaction fees) generated by your node.
- **The Catch:** You do not *own* the 100,000 QUA principal. You cannot sell it. You only own the yield you generate from it.

## 3. Uptime Requirements & Calculations
To earn the 5,000 QUA grant AND the 100,000 QUA delegation, you must maintain your Katenet node.

| Katenet Uptime | TGE Token Grant | Foundation Delegation |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **90% - 100%** | 5,000 QUA | 100,000 QUA (Full Delegation) |
| **70% - 89%** | 2,500 QUA | 50,000 QUA (Partial Delegation) |
| **Below 70%** | 0 QUA | 0 QUA (Disqualified) |

*Note: If you only receive a 50,000 QUA delegation, you will need to acquire or attract the remaining 50,000 QUA from other delegators to hit the 100,000 minimum required to run a Mainnet node.*

## 4. Future Protocol Upgrades (Sortition)
Quanta is currently operating as a Delegated Proof of Stake (DPoS) network where the top 21 validators by stake are chosen. In the future, the protocol may upgrade to a random selection model (Sortition). 

**Your guarantee:** Regardless of future consensus upgrades, if you earn the Foundation Delegation during Katenet, the Foundation guarantees to keep your node delegated for 1 full year, ensuring you remain eligible to participate and earn block rewards in whatever model is active.

## 5. Disqualification (Zero Tolerance)
You will forfeit all rewards and delegations if your node:
1. **Double-signs a block (Equivocation):** This is a severe network offense.
2. **Fails to patch:** You must apply critical Katenet software updates within 48 hours of release.
